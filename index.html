About me✨

My name is Nora, I live in Hungary (mid Europe). I am a mother of 2 baby potatoes (2 and 4 yo). I started altering in December of 2024 for fun.

I am not a full time alterist, so I usually paint at nights (10 pm- 2 am) when my kids are sleeping, the house is quiet enough and I have enough energy to enjoy painting.

My style: I love every type of art and I like challenges, so I paint almost everything, except for realistic portrait for now.
